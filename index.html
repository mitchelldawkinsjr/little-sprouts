Room to grow

Choose a learner and make their next adventure just right.

Each learner keeps their own name, math band, levels, sight words, and garden buddy. This device can hold 8 learners.

Garden buddy

Math games follow this band, not the shape and color levels. Malik starts in preschool, Micah in kindergarten, and Mitch in 4th grade.

Arcade play belongs to this learner, preschool or anyone. Dirt ride, Sky flight, Garden walk, Moon shot, Shooting hoops, Stick bridge, Tilt maze, Garden chase, Sprout hop, Toast pop, Island glide, Market walk, Lily cross, Mole pop, Ghost bust, Stacker, Light the lamp, Cube dash, and Catch the light run without questions. Shape, word, count, and color games still ask.

These levels apply in every play style.

Level 1: 3 choices, circle / square / triangle, four colors, and 3 shapes to remember. Word games show the word on screen.

Level 2: 4 choices, more shapes and colors, and 4 shapes to remember. Word and letter games hide the word on screen; the voice still says it. Letter games add 1 extra letter.

Level 3: up to 6 choices, the full shape and color sets, and 5 shapes to remember. Letter games add 2 extra letters. A word game cannot show more choices than there are words in the list.

Preschool math: count 1–5, then 8, then 10. Find numbers and say which is more. Hunt still uses real objects, up to 10.

Kindergarten math: count 1–5, then 10, then 20. Level 1 finds numbers through 5. Before, after, and which is more come next, within 10, then hops that add or take away within 10. Level 1 spelling uses 2 and 3 letter words. Levels 2 and 3 add 4 letter words. Longer words are spelled only when they are typed into the sight-word list.

4th grade math is all multiplication and multiples. Count & clap: arrays (level 1), the times tables listed in the question sheet and missing factors (level 2), then the double-digit and tens rows that are turned on (level 3). The sheet starts with tables through 12 and a cap at 12, so both numbers in a math fact stay at 12 or under. 10 × a number still counts, because 10 is not above 12. Put off on the cap row to allow facts above 12, including double digits and the 20s or 30s. Clear off on a 20s or 30s row to add that set once the cap is off. Number path: skip-counting and multiples of 2, 5, and 10 (level 1), the other tables and any tens you turned on (level 2), then factors, common multiples, and primes (level 3). In Move they skip-count with a jump, clap, or step for each multiple; in Hunt they build groups with real objects or check on a calendar. Hints give a strategy, not the answer.

Skills & progress

Set each skill independently. “Flourishing” means at least 80% correct on first tries across 10 or more questions at that level. This is a practice indicator, not a formal assessment. The line under each skill matches the level selected above it.

Separate words with commas. Use your teacher’s list. Add at least 3 different words. Letters only, with at most one apostrophe, up to 12 letters. Kindergarten spelling stays on 2, 3, and 4 letter words. A longer word is spelled only when it is typed here. Build a word uses words longer than one letter when it can. Preschool does not spell. Those games use numbers, shapes, and colors. Words from the question sheet are used instead when that sheet has at least 3 words for this learner. Rows marked grade4, preschool, or kindergarten are used only for that math band. A word on the 4th grade list stays there, even when the shared list also has it.

Gas race

Gas race stays on the screen in every play style. The tank starts full. Steering into the right answer fills it again. A wrong lane ends the race, and so does an empty tank. The score is how far the car traveled. Every learner on this device shares one distance board. Math facts follow the math band and the Gas race level. Sight words follow the list above. Backroad rally

These choices belong to the learner selected above. Spelling says the word, then the learner taps or types the letters in order. Preschool has no spelling: the questions are numbers, shapes, and colors. Match asks for a shape, color, or word. 4th grade math starts with single-digit facts and the 12s. Choose “Open it up” for double-digit facts, tens, factors, and primes. That still follows the question sheet, so a cap of 12 keeps the facts at 12 and under until the cap row is turned off. Preschool and kindergarten math uses that learner’s counting and number path. Questions are spread along the stage. Five right answers, and the learner can drive the rest of the way to the finish. A 10-question round asks seven, and a 15-question round asks eight. Sky shot uses this same 4th-grade math menu. Sight words still take turns with those facts, and the Sky shot level still sets how many rocks and enemy ships. Sky flight uses these same choices. The plane holds its height and the learner steers through the gate with the answer. A wrong gate slows the plane and turns it back, and that question stays until the right gate is flown through. After five right answers, the learner gets two minutes of free flight. The plane keeps flying where they look. Forward flies faster, back flies backward, and the side buttons slide. Hitting the ground ends the flight. Dirt ride uses them too. Hold the gas and the bike pulls away. Lean back to lift the front wheel, and lean forward to bring the nose down. Ride the hills and land on both wheels. A question comes after a longer stretch of trail. A right answer says good, and the ride continues. A wrong answer speeds the bike up. Level 1 stays upright. Level 3 lets the learner tilt in the air.

Question sheet

Checking the question sheet…

The default sheet is included with the app. Columns: learner, game, item, extra. game is words, count, hunt, or facts. Leave learner blank, or write *, for every learner. Write grade4, preschool, or kindergarten to limit words to that math band. For count, item is the name and extra is the picture. For hunt, item is one object and extra is the plural. For facts, item is a times table from 2 to 12, or double, 10s, 20s, or 30s, or cap. Put off in extra to leave that row out, and clear off to add the 20s or 30s. The cap row starts at 12, so math facts stay at 12 and under. Put off on cap, or write a higher number, to allow facts above 12. A row named above with off does the same as a cap of 12. A list in the Google Sheet replaces that same list from the default. Facts rows the Google Sheet does not have yet, such as double and 20s, are added from the default sheet. Add a hide column with yes to turn a row off. New default rows are copied into the Google Sheet about once an hour after the sheet script is installed.

Moving & real-world play

Move and Hunt turn each game into a movement or find-it activity, and a grown-up confirms the answer. Print floor cards for Move.

Reach play finds the child’s hands with an on-device pose model so they can hold a hand on an answer until it fills. Stand far enough back that both shoulders and both hands stay in the picture. Video is processed in this browser only; it is never recorded, saved or uploaded. The pose model downloads from Google the first time, and the browser will ask for camera permission. This choice is shared by every learner.

Settings and progress stay in this browser. They do not sync between devices. Parent corner is a grown-up check, not a security lock.
