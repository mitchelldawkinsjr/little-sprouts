Whoever has the bee is it. Tag the other buddy to pass the bee along. Open gift boxes for power-ups.

Climb the logs one step at a time, and bounce on the red mushrooms. Gift boxes hold power-ups: ⚡ speed, 🫧 a bubble that blocks a tag, 🚀 super jump, 🧊 freeze the other buddy, and 🐝 bee magnet. Answer a question right to open one.
