Moonlight Swing

Swing through a glowing rink and jump for high gems.

Move with arrows, WASD, or the mouse. Hold Shift or controller X for the rope. Press Space, A, or click to jump off.

Drag a thumb on the left to steer. Hold 🪢 to swing, and tap ↗ to jump off and grab things in the air.

Gold gems are points. In learning play, touch the floating answer. Clear a mode to unlock the next.
