Moonlight Swing

Skate a glowing rink on a stretchy leash and hop for high gems.

Point the mouse where your buddy should skate, or slide the leash with arrows, WASD, or the stick. Click, Space, or A to hop. Faster skating means bigger spins.

Drag a thumb on the left to pull the leash. Tap ↗ to hop and spin for things in the air.

Gold gems are points. In learning play, touch the floating answer. Clear a mode to unlock the next.
