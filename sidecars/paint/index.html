Paint Sphere

You are inside a hollow world. A question appears, and each answer floats on one half of the shell. Look for five seconds, then paint for ten. Only paint on the right answer scores.

  • Mouse or right stick to look. Click or RT to throw paint
  • WASD or left stick to run, Shift or LB to sprint, Space or A to jump
  • Look for 5 seconds. Then throw paint for 10 seconds
  • Start on a controller plays without the mouse
  • Paint the half with the answer you think is right
  • The train on the middle circle sets you down beside the track
