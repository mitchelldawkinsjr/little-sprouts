# Pebble roll

A recreation of [Rock game - threeJS](https://codepen.io/aado29/pen/qzLVPr) by Alberto Diaz (aado29).
The pale spherical world, bright faceted pines, three-lane hops, score, and collision burst follow
the reference. The implementation recreates them on Little Sprouts' local Three.js.
The shared garden buddy ball, checkpoint questions, retry and shield reward, controller and touch
input, and offline packaging are Little Sprouts adaptations.
