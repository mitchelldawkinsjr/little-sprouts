Little Sprouts

Playful learning, made for little minds.

Little Sprouts is a collection of learning and arcade games. Learner profiles and progress stay in your browser. Save games over Wi-Fi to play on the go.

Open Little Sprouts

Anonymous usage counts

Little Sprouts uses Google Analytics to count visits and which games get played, so we can fix problems and improve the games. It never sends a learner's name, grade or answers, and advertising features are turned off. Learner profiles and progress stay in your browser.
